## Summary

**Inference** Three jurisdictions look most likely to reward a US investor: South Korea (won stablecoin law pending, Naver Financial plus Upbit, EWY at 7.6x P/E), Kazakhstan (state digital tenge, KSPI at 6.75x forward), and Brazil (post-election valuation reset, highest adoption). [Low] Basis is valuation plus regulatory catalyst, not return data.

**Inference** Three most likely to cause losses: Argentina (cepo still binds companies), Nigeria (repatriation history, 2027 election, conflicting flow data), Russia (sanctions; US persons cannot invest). [Low to Moderate]

**Fact** Brazil won the adoption ranking and also had the largest market shock: Bovespa hit a circuit breaker and the real fell about 12% on 2026-10-26 after Lula's re-election. Regulation also tightened (BCB Res. 561 bars stablecoin settlement in regulated cross-border eFX from 2026-10-01).

**Fact** CBDCs are weak where measured: India retail e-rupee in circulation fell 24% to Rs 771.7 crore (2026-03-31). Russia and Kazakhstan are the state-push exceptions. Cambodia's Bakong is the strongest adoption evidence (1.33B transactions in 2025).

**Recommendation** Treat country ETFs and NU, MELI, KSPI as macro and equity bets with a thin stablecoin link. Size small relative to the 40% drawdown limit. [Low]
