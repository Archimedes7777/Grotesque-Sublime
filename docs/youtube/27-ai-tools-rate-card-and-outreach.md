# Rate card and sponsor outreach template

**Purpose:** the two documents the disclosure policy's pre-deal process requires (`24-ai-tools-disclosure-policy.md`, section 5, steps 2 and 3). **Status:** template. No rate is quoted until the channel has five published videos with measured views. Written 2026-11-06.

## 1. How the rate is set

Sponsors price on views, not subscribers. The rate for an integration is:

> median views of the last five long-form videos, measured at 30 days, divided by 1,000, multiplied by the niche CPM

Niche CPM for B2B software, AI tools, and developer tools runs $30 to $80 per 1,000 views in 2026 benchmarks **[Estimate; see `01-niche-research.md` section 3.2]**. A new channel quotes at the low end and raises the CPM with evidence (audience profession data from YouTube Studio, click-through on affiliate links, comment quality).

Worked examples, all **[Estimate]**:

| Median 30-day views | CPM used | 60 to 90 second integration | Dedicated video (sponsored) | Description link only |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2,000 | $30 | $60 | not offered | $25 |
| 10,000 | $35 | $350 | $1,000 | $100 |
| 25,000 | $40 | $1,000 | $3,000 | $300 |
| 50,000 | $45 | $2,250 | $6,500 | $600 |
| 100,000 | $50 | $5,000 | $14,000 | $1,200 |

Rules behind the table:

- Under about 5,000 median views, an integration is not worth a sponsor's time or the channel's credibility. Offer affiliate relationships and product access instead, and say so plainly.
- A dedicated video is priced at about three times an integration because it displaces a workflow video and carries "(sponsored)" in the title.
- A professional audience justifies the upper half of the CPM range. The audience evidence is the pitch; see section 3.
- Shorts are not sold separately. A Short cut from a sponsored video that includes sponsored frames carries the on-screen label and is included in the integration price.
- Governance videos are never sold. If a sponsor asks for one, the answer is the next workflow slot.

## 2. What is included and what is not

| Included in an integration | Not included |
|---|---|
| 60 to 90 seconds, placed after the first result reveal and before the build (about 1:00), clearly labeled on screen for its duration | Approval of the script, the conclusions, or the cut |
| Spoken disclosure at the start of the video and in the description's first three lines | Any claim the owner cannot verify by using the product |
| One tracked link and one code in the description, for 12 months | Placement inside a failure segment or in a governance video |
| One round of factual corrections to the sponsor's own product description | Exclusivity beyond 30 days in the same product category |
| A screenshot report at 7 and 30 days with views, click-through, and link clicks | Audience data beyond what YouTube Studio shows in aggregate |

## 5. Outreach email (first contact)

Subject: `[Channel name]: AI workflows for technical and clinical professionals, integration proposal`

> Hello [name],
>
> I run [channel name], a YouTube channel where a certified biomedical equipment technician uses AI tools on real technical work and shows the results, including what goes wrong. [Product] is part of my own workflow: [one sentence on the specific task it appears in, for example "it is the tool I use to draft preventive maintenance procedures from manufacturer manuals in the video published on [date]"].
>
> The audience is working professionals in regulated and technical jobs, mostly in the US, UK, Canada, and Australia. Current figures, measured in YouTube Studio: median [N] views per video at 30 days, [X] percent average viewed, [Y] percent of viewers in those four countries. The evidence sheet is attached.
>
> I am proposing a 60 to 90 second integration in an upcoming workflow video where [product] is already the tool being used, at $[rate] (based on [N] median views at $[CPM] CPM). The integration is disclosed on screen and spoken at the start, carries one tracked link for 12 months, and includes a 7-day and 30-day performance report.
>
> Two things I want to be clear about before you reply. First, sponsors do not review or approve my scripts or conclusions, and if [product] loses a comparison in a video, the video says so. Second, I do not place sponsors in my security and governance videos, which is a quarter of the channel. Those rules are why the audience trusts the recommendations, and they are not negotiable.
>
> If that fits, I can send the written agreement and the next available slot. If a product-access or affiliate arrangement is a better starting point, I am open to that as well.
>
> Regards,
> [Name]
> [Channel link] [Evidence sheet attached]

## 6. Follow-up (one, after 10 business days)

> Hello [name], following up once on the integration proposal I sent on [date] for [channel name]. The next open slot is the week of [date]. If the timing or format does not fit, a reply saying so is welcome and I will not follow up again. Regards, [Name]

No second follow-up. No follow-up by direct message on any platform.

## 7. Agreement terms to send (summary for the written agreement)

1. Deliverable: one integration of 60 to 90 seconds in the video scheduled for [date], placed after the first result reveal, labeled on screen for its duration.
2. Disclosure: spoken at the start of the video and in the first three lines of the description; YouTube paid-promotion flag set.
3. Editorial independence: the sponsor has no right of review or approval over any part of the video, including the integration's wording beyond factual accuracy about the product. If the sponsor requests a change to a finding or conclusion, the agreement terminates and any payment received is returned.
4. Claims: the owner makes only claims verifiable by using the product; the sponsor supplies a factual product description and pricing, which the owner may paraphrase.
5. Link and code: one tracked link and one code in the description for 12 months.
6. Reporting: a screenshot report at 7 and 30 days.
7. Payment: [amount], [net 15 or 50 percent on signing and 50 percent on publication], by invoice.
8. Exclusivity: none beyond 30 days in the same product category.
9. Content retention: the video stays published; the sponsor may not request removal; the owner may add a correction note if a product claim becomes outdated.
10. Conflict: the sponsor confirms it is not a current or bidding supplier to the owner's employer.

Have the agreement reviewed by an attorney before first use **[recommended; this summary is not legal advice]**.
