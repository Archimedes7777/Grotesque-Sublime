# Step 2 output: 12-week channel plan for "governed AI workflows for regulated and technical work"

**Produced:** 2026-11-06 from Step 2 of `10-prompts-ai-tools.md`, using the Step 1 evaluation and the competitor audit. **Time budget assumed: 8 hours per week.** Everything below scales with that number.

**One correction to the Step 1 goal.** The context block asked for 30 long-form videos in 90 days. At 8 hours a week, that is not honest. A 10-minute screen-capture workflow video with a measured result costs about 6.5 hours (section 4). The plan below delivers **12 long-form videos and 24 Shorts in 12 weeks**. Thirty long-form videos in 90 days needs about 16 hours a week or a shift to 5-minute videos, which would drop below the mid-roll threshold and the practice-lane length norm.

## 1. Positioning

**Positioning statement:** Real job tasks done with AI agents, shown working and shown failing, with the guardrails a regulated workplace needs.

**Channel description (under 1,000 characters):**

> I am a CBET-certified biomedical equipment technician with eight years in hospital technology and a background in AI and prompt engineering. On this channel I use AI tools and agents on real technical work: service manuals, maintenance procedures, work-order backlogs, incident reports, regulatory lookups, and weekly reporting. Every video shows the task start to finish, measures the result, and shows what the AI got wrong and how I caught it. One video in four is about doing this safely: what never to paste into an AI tool, how to vet a tool or an agent skill before you install it, how permissions work, and what to do when something goes wrong. No hype, no tool of the week, no income claims. Sponsors and affiliate links are always disclosed and never change a recommendation. If you work in a job with rules and want AI to carry some of the load without creating a new risk, this channel is for you.

## 2. Content pillars

| Pillar | Share | Length | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| Worked workflows | 45 percent (5 of 12) | 9 to 14 min | One real task, start to finish, result measured on screen, one failure shown and fixed |
| Governed AI | 25 percent (3 of 12) | 8 to 12 min | Data boundaries, vetting, permissions, incident handling; the series mapped in `25-ai-tools-governance-series.md` |
| Head-to-head on one task | 20 percent (3 of 12) | 9 to 12 min | Two or three tools, same job, same rubric, scores shown |
| Viewer problem of the week | 10 percent (1 of 12, plus Shorts) | 3 to 5 min, and Shorts | Answers to submitted tasks |

Shorts: two per long-form video, cut from the result reveal and the failure moment. They exist for discovery; they earn 3 to 14 percent of long-form RPM.

## 4. Weekly hours (8 total)

| Step | Hours | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Research and task prep (pick the task, gather de-identified material, run it once off camera) | 1.5 | The off-camera run finds the failure moment before recording |
| Script and outline | 1.0 | Result first, build second, failure third, fix fourth, steps last |
| Recording (screen capture plus voiceover) | 1.5 | Record in one sitting; redaction checked before recording starts |
| Editing | 2.0 | Zoom and cursor emphasis, cuts, on-screen result numbers |
| Thumbnail, title, description, chapters, disclosure block | 0.5 | Templates in section 6 |
| Shorts (two cuts) | 0.5 | Vertical crop of the reveal and the failure |
| Review, publish by hand, 24-hour and weekly analytics entry | 1.0 | The analytics template is Step 4 |

Research for governance videos is lower (the material exists in the repository); the saved hour goes to the next week's task prep.

## 5. Retention structure for every workflow video

| Time | Segment | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:20 | The finished result on screen, one sentence on the task, one sentence on what broke | Earns the click-through and sets the promise |
| 0:20 to 1:00 | The task as it exists in the job, the material used, what is redacted and why | Credibility and the governance habit |
| 1:00 to 5:00 | The build, step by step, at the speed a viewer can follow | The value; chapter markers here |
| 5:00 to 7:00 | The failure: what the AI got wrong, how it was caught | The honesty signal and the retention spike |
| 7:00 to 8:30 | The fix and the measured result (minutes, errors, rows, hours per week) | The thumbnail number, proved |
| 8:30 to end | The reproducible steps on screen, the disclosure line, one next task | Comments and subscription without asking for them |

Governance videos use the same shape with "the risk in one sentence" replacing the result and "how to check you did it right" replacing the measurement.

## 6. Templates

**Title register:** declarative and specific. "Writing a PM procedure with AI in 20 minutes, checked against the standard." Never "insane", "secret", "you won't believe", or a bare question.

**Thumbnail:** the number (minutes, errors, hours per week) or a two-column before-and-after, with an overlay of three words or fewer. No face required. Same typeface and color pair every time.

**Description block (reused):**

> Task: [one line]. Tools used: [list]. Material: [de-identified, owner-controlled, or public source]. Result: [the number]. What went wrong: [one line].
> Chapters: [timestamps]
> Disclosure: [the current line from `24-ai-tools-disclosure-policy.md`]
> Steps and templates: [link to the repository or download]
> Nothing in this video is medical, legal, or financial advice. Device service requires manufacturer training and institutional authorization.

**AI-content label:** required if a synthetic narration voice is used. The channel plans a human voice; record the decision per video.

## 7. Weekly action checklist (by hand)

- [ ] Monday: choose the task; confirm the material is de-identified or owner-controlled; run it once off camera; note the failure moment.
- [ ] Tuesday: script from the retention structure; write the three title options; sketch the thumbnail number.
- [ ] Wednesday: record in one sitting; check the redaction list before pressing record.
- [ ] Thursday: edit; cut two Shorts; build the thumbnail.
- [ ] Friday: write description from the block; add chapters and disclosure; run the pre-publish checklist (no confidential data, result shown, failure shown, disclosure present, AI-label decision recorded); publish by hand.
- [ ] Saturday: record 24-hour numbers in the Step 4 weekly template; reply to comments by hand.
- [ ] Sunday: nothing. The budget is 8 hours.

## 8. Milestones and decision points

| Week | Check | If missed |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Four videos live; average view duration above 40 percent on at least two | Shorten the build segment; move the result earlier |
| 8 | Click-through rate above 4 percent on at least three videos; first viewer task submitted | Rework thumbnails to a single number; ask for tasks in the end segment |
| 12 | Subscribers above 500 (early access tier) or clear month-over-month growth; one governance video in the top three by views | If governance underperforms, keep it at 25 percent anyway for the first 24 videos; it is the moat and the sponsor pitch |

Monetization is not in this plan. Step 7 runs after 30 videos or 90 days of data, and the disclosure policy must exist before any sponsor conversation.
