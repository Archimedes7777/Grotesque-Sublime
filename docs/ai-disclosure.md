# AI disclosure statement

**Purpose:** say once, clearly, how the art is made, so followers trust it and platform rules are met. The statement must stay **true**: update it if the process changes.

## What the statement covers

- AI image tools generate the starting images.
- A person directs the prompts, selects, edits, and finishes every piece, and adds the brand name by hand.
- Each platform's own AI-content label is applied where required.

## Bio line (all platforms)

"AI-assisted, human-edited."

Short enough for every bio limit. It sits under the brand line in `docs/bio-and-link-plan.md`.

## Pinned post (Instagram, X) or channel section (YouTube)

**Title or first line:** "How this art is made"

**Body draft:**

"Every piece here starts with AI image tools and finishes by hand.

I write the prompts, generate many versions, choose the one that stays, then edit, recolor, and add the name myself. The AI keeps misspelling it, so that part is always manual.

I label AI-generated work using each platform's tools. Nothing here shows real people, and nothing is presented as a photograph of a real event.

Questions about the process are welcome in the comments."

**Image for the pin:** a 3-slide carousel: a reject, the keeper, and the finished piece with the wordmark.

## YouTube "About" paragraph

"ActivelyPsychotic makes dark, satirical, surreal art with AI image tools and human editing. Each piece is directed, selected, edited, and finished by hand. AI-generated content is disclosed using YouTube's altered or synthetic content setting where it applies."

## Per-post short form

When a caption has room: "Made with AI tools, edited by hand." Not required on every post if the platform label is applied and the bio states it.

## Rules

- **Never** claim a piece is hand-drawn, photographed, or "no AI" when AI was used.
- **Never** remove image metadata to avoid a platform's AI label.
- If you later sell work, keep the same disclosure on product pages (see `docs/launch-plan.md`).
- Keep records of prompts, selections, and edits; they also support human-authorship claims (`docs/trademark-plan.md`, related risks).
