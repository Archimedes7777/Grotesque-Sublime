1. Pick a master piece. Create the art without any text in it, then add the exact wordmark `BOB FRAPPLES` as an overlay (AI models misspell it).
2. Adapt it using the platform columns. Copy the caption draft, then edit it in your own voice.
3. Run the pre-post checklist (bottom). Publish by hand.
4. After 24 hours, record the numbers in the weekly review table.

## Platform notes

- **Instagram:** about 125 characters show before "more", so front-load the line. Max 5 hashtags.
- **TikTok:** hook in the first second. About 150 characters show before "more". Turn on the **AI-generated content** toggle at upload for realistic synthetic content.
- **YouTube:** answer the **altered or synthetic content** question when uploading. Titles show about 70 characters. For Shorts, use 1 to 5 hashtags.
- **X:** 280 characters on a standard account. Images do the work.
