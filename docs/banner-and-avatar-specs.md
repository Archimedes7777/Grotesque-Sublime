# Banner and avatar specs

Sizes from third-party guides fetched 2026-10-31; the official help pages did not appear in search. Tagged **[Moderate]** where sources agree, **[Unverified]** where they conflict. Upload once at the recommended size, then check on both a phone and a desktop.

## Design rules (all platforms)

- **Wordmark:** `ACTIVELYPSYCHOTIC`, added by overlay in Canva or Figma, never drawn by the AI. Check the spelling letter by letter.
- **Palette:** void black `#05060A` base, bone ivory `#F0ECE0` text, one signature accent (blood burgundy `#8E1B3A` or toxic neon green `#39FF14`). See `.agents/product-marketing.md`.
- **No Apple logo silhouettes, no franchise references, no real faces.**
- Export PNG for text-heavy art, high-quality JPG for photographic art; keep under the file limit.

## Avatar (profile picture)

Use the **minimal dripping skull** (image 11) everywhere, so the four accounts read as one brand.

Prepare one square master (1080 x 1080) with the skull centered inside a circle about 80 percent of the width, then export the other sizes from it.

## YouTube banner

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Recommended size | **2560 x 1440** (16:9) **[Moderate]** |
| Minimum size | 2048 x 1152 **[Moderate]** |
| Safe area for text and logo | About **1546 x 423** centered, at 2560 x 1440 (about 1235 x 338 at the minimum size) **[Moderate; guides vary]** |
| File limit | About 6 MB **[Moderate]** |

**Layout:**
- Wordmark and the one-line tagline ("Dark satirical AI art") inside the centered safe area only.
- Fill the full 2560 x 1440 with art (a cosmic or neon piece); everything outside the safe area is decoration that TVs show and phones crop.
- Check in YouTube Studio's preview for desktop, mobile, and TV before saving.

## X header

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Size | **1500 x 500** (3:1) **[Moderate]** |
| File limit | 2 MB to 5 MB depending on the source **[Unverified]**; stay under 2 MB to be safe |
| Cropping | Up to about 60 px may be cropped from top and bottom on some screens **[Moderate]** |
| Avatar overlap | The profile picture covers the **bottom-left** area on desktop **[Moderate]** |

**Layout:**
- Keep the wordmark center or right of center, away from the bottom-left corner.
- Keep everything important within the middle 380 px of height.

## Instagram and TikTok

Neither has a banner. Brand consistency there comes from the avatar, the bio (`docs/bio-and-link-plan.md`), and the first 9 posts of the grid. On Instagram, pin the mark reveal (piece 1) and two strong pieces from different style families.

## Checklist before uploading

- [ ] Wordmark spelled `ACTIVELYPSYCHOTIC`
- [ ] Text inside the safe area on YouTube; away from the bottom-left on X
- [ ] Checked on phone and desktop previews
- [ ] File under the size limit
- [ ] Uploaded inside the official app or site, never through a link someone sent you
