## How this calendar uses your results

Weeks 1 and 2 had no data yet. Weeks 3 and 4 adapt to it. Before Fri Nov 13, fill in the 24-hour results tables in the four draft packs, then make the three decisions below. Each master piece marked **[ADAPT]** changes based on them.

| Decision | How to read your results | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Winning style family** | Which family earned the most saves and shares per view: heritage tattoo flash (pieces 1), neon horror (2, 3), blacklight psychedelic (6, 9), cosmic sublime (5, 7) | Pieces 11, 14, and 17 use the winning family |
| **2. Winning format** | Carousel vs Reel vs single image on Instagram, by saves per view | Each [ADAPT] piece uses the winning format on Instagram |
| **3. Did the creature get a name?** | Comments on piece 6 | If yes, the creature becomes a recurring character in pieces 13 and 18 |

If results are too thin to call (under about 300 views per post), keep the week 1 and 2 pattern and decide again after week 4.
