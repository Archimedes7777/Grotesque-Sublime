# Two-minute pre-post check

Run this before **every** post, on every platform. It takes about two minutes once it is habit. If any box fails, fix it or do not post.

## 1. The image (30 seconds)

- [ ] **Brand text spelled right.** Any visible name reads exactly `ACTIVELYPSYCHOTIC` or `BOB FRAPPLES`. No "FRAPPPLES". If the AI drew it, cover it and add it by overlay
- [ ] **Watermark present:** a small `ACTIVELYPSYCHOTIC`
- [ ] **No Apple Inc. logo** or anything close to its silhouette
- [ ] **No other brands, franchise characters, or names** in the image
- [ ] **No real people's faces** presented as real people
- [ ] **No banknote-style design** printed as anything other than clearly digital parody
- [ ] **Screenshots cropped:** no phone status bar, notifications, or other apps visible

## 2. The words (30 seconds)

- [ ] **No product, price, shop link, or "limited" wording.** The shop is not live
- [ ] **No claims you cannot prove** ("first", "only", "best")
- [ ] **Hook in the first line,** before the "more" cut-off
- [ ] **Hashtags within limits:** up to 5 on Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts; 1 or 2 on X
- [ ] **No em dashes, no "it's not X, it's Y", no engagement bait** ("Agree?", "Thoughts?")
- [ ] **Satire punches at ideas,** not at real people or at people with mental illness

## 3. The platform settings (30 seconds)

- [ ] **AI-content label set:** TikTok's AI-generated toggle; YouTube's altered or synthetic content answer; on Instagram, do not strip metadata to avoid Meta's label
- [ ] **Alt text written** (Instagram: Advanced settings; X: Add description)
- [ ] **Audio cleared:** from the platform's library, or your own

## 4. Safety (30 seconds)

- [ ] **Posting from your own device,** in the official app
- [ ] **No tool or agent was given** a password, session, or code to post this
- [ ] **No link in the post** that you did not type or check yourself

## After posting

- [ ] Note the post in the 24-hour results table of its draft pack
- [ ] Reply to real comments yourself; do not automate likes, follows, or replies

If something goes wrong after posting (a wrong name, a missed label, a scam reply), edit or delete the post and see `docs/incident-response.md` if it involves your account's security.
