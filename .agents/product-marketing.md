# Product marketing context: ActivelyPsychotic

Read by the `social`, `image`, and `video` skills before they ask questions. Lines marked **[CONFIRM]** are assumptions drafted by Claude; the owner should correct them. This file contains no credentials and must never contain any.

## Identity

- **Brand:** ActivelyPsychotic, an AI art project and creator brand.
- **What it is:** Original AI-assisted visual art (stills and short-form video), published as a body of work rather than one-off posts.
- **Aesthetic:** Grotesque and sublime: the beautiful-disturbing tension, surreal and uncanny imagery, high craft. **[CONFIRM]** (inferred from the repository name)
- **Goal:** Grow an engaged audience across Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, and X. Secondary goals **[CONFIRM]**: commissions, prints, collaborations, community.

## Audience [CONFIRM]

- Fans of surreal, dark, and horror-adjacent art; digital art and AI art communities; other creators.
- Values: originality, visible process, a consistent artistic voice. They punish low-effort "AI slop".

## Voice

- Confident, dark-witted, art-first. Speak about the work, not about the tools.
- Short, concrete captions. Never sensationalize real mental illness, and never mock people living with it. The brand name is an aesthetic, not a diagnosis. **[CONFIRM]**
- Follow the `social` skill's "No AI Tells" rules. No em dashes in posts.

## Content pillars [CONFIRM percentages]

| Pillar | Share | Examples |

## Platforms

| Platform | Primary format | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Reels (9:16) and 4:5 carousels | Saves and shares weigh heavily |
| TikTok | 9:16 short video, 15 to 45 s | Hook in the first second; process reveals |
| YouTube | Shorts plus occasional long-form process videos | Searchable titles |
| X | Single images and short threads | Community and art-sharing hashtags, sparingly |

## Non-negotiable rules (from SECURITY.md)

- **Drafts only.** Agents create drafts; the owner reviews and publishes every post.
- **No engagement automation:** no automated likes, follows, comments, or DMs.
- **AI disclosure:** apply each platform's AI-generated content label where its rules require one (YouTube and TikTok require disclosure for realistic synthetic content; Meta and TikTok also auto-label from C2PA metadata). Check current rules before each campaign.
- **Rights:** no likenesses of real private individuals; no other artists' names as style prompts in published captions; keep prompt and source records for each published piece.
- Never request or store account passwords, session cookies, or tokens in this repository.
