## Rules (from SECURITY.md and legal review of the references)

- **Drafts only.** Agents create drafts; the owner reviews and publishes every post. No automated likes, follows, comments, or DMs. Never request or store account passwords, session cookies, or tokens.
- **AI disclosure.** Apply each platform's AI-generated content label where it requires one. Do not strip image metadata to avoid labels.
- **Scarcity claims must be true.** "Limited edition", "no reprints", and "only N available" are factual claims. Do not publish them until the edition size is real and recorded.
- **Apple Inc. trademark.** Several references use Apple's actual logo silhouette (rainbow apple, apple on a tombstone). Do not use these for posts, products, or ads. The skull-in-apple mark is the brand's own.
- **Currency lookalikes.** The banknote-style design should stay a digital parody. Do not print it at or near real banknote size or color, and do not add serial numbers or seals.
- **Third-party references.** The PDF's "Upsidedown Place" line points at a famous franchise. Avoid franchise names and imagery in captions and merchandise.
- **Copyright.** Material generated purely by AI prompts may not be protected by copyright in the US (Copyright Office guidance, 2025) [Moderate]. Keep records of prompts, selections, edits, and paintovers that show human authorship, especially for anything sold as exclusive.
- **Mental-health sensitivity.** Skull and death imagery is fine. Do not glorify self-harm, and follow each platform's rules on mental-health content.
- **Respect cultural traditions.** Skull imagery is not Día de Muertos imagery. Do not borrow sugar-skull styling for sales posts.
